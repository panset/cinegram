# Plans

Decision records, kept for the reasoning rather than the conclusions. They are
dated and they are **not corrected as the code moves on** — a record that gets
edited to match what happened stops being evidence of what was decided.

Nothing here is on the website. It used to be, in the sense that the files sat
in the folder GitHub Pages served and were downloadable as raw Markdown; when
the site moved to Zensical they would have become rendered pages, describing an
arrangement that no longer exists as though it were current. They live here
instead.
